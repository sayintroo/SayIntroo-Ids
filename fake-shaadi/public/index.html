FAQ

Sawaal jawaab

Shaadi sach mein fake hai na? 😅

Haan bestie, 100% fake. Koi pheron ka paperwork nahi. Bas vibes, dance, khaana aur ek real cause.

Mera paisa kahan jaata hai?

Seedha NGO ke UPI pe — . Hum (organisers) beech mein paisa touch bhi nahi karte. Jo UPI ID upar dikh rahi hai, wo NGO ki hai.

Leaderboard pe naam kaise aayega?

Pay karne ke baad venue pe kisi volunteer ko screenshot dikhao. Wo naam ya Insta handle add kar dega aur leaderboard turant update ho jayega — stage screen pe bhi. Naam nahi chahiye? Bol do "Anonymous".

Minimum kitna dena hai?

Koi minimum nahi. ₹51 ki chai bhi chalegi. Dil bada hona chahiye, amount nahi.

UPI app nahi khul raha?

Kuch browsers (jaise Insta ke andar wala) UPI links block karte hain. UPI ID copy karo aur GPay/PhonePe/Paytm mein manually pay karo. Ya page ko Chrome/Safari mein kholo.

Tax receipt / 80G milega?

Ye NGO pe depend karta hai. Venue pe volunteer se poochho — hum NGO se connect karwa denge.

Shagun card ka kya karun?

Download karo, Insta story pe daalo, event ko tag karo. Jitne zyada log dekhenge, utna zyada shagun NGO tak jayega. Main character energy 💅
